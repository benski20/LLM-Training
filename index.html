    What's under the hood
    
The training loop

    
Every practice round, the model reads 64 snippets of text, guesses each next character, measures how surprised it was by the real answers, and nudges its 5 million numbers to be less surprised. On a T4 it does several thousand rounds in 6 minutes.

  

  
  

    
Next-token guessing
The only thing an LLM is trained to do. Everything else emerges from it.

    
Tokens
Text becomes numbers. Ours uses single characters. Big models use word pieces.

    
Surprise (loss)
How unlikely the model thought the right answer was. Training pushes it down.

    
Parameters
The adjustable numbers that hold everything the model knows. Theirs: about 5 million.

    
Attention
Each position decides which earlier text matters for its guess.

    
Temperature
Writing means rolling weighted dice. Low is safe and repetitive. High is wild.

  







  

    Reading the notebook dashboard
    
What to point at

  

  

    

      
        
Confusion"Like picking from N characters." Starts near 65 (blind guessing) and should end around 4–5. It's exp(loss), but you don't need to say that.

        
Stage🍼 babbling → 🔤 alphabet → 🧩 word chunks → 📖 real words → 🎭 style. A rough guide based on confusion.

        
Surprise curvePurple line: practice text. Orange dots: secret test text. Both falling means real learning. If only the line keeps falling, it's memorising (overfitting).

        
Next letterIts guesses after "…yonder window brea". It ends up torn between k (the real answer) and t ("breath"). Both are sensible guesses.

        
AttentionWhich earlier letters it looked at to make that guess. Mostly the current word, sometimes further back.

        
SamplesNewest first. The bottom one is from before any training.
